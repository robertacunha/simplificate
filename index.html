What you can hire me for

For my clients and in my in-house roles, I have built paid, organic and lifecycle into one engine for profitable growth, with total marketing spend read against each company’s margins.

Full package

Growth strategy and execution

The full growth methodology, from strategy to execution across acquisition, conversion and retention. If your team runs execution in house, the strategy and plan are also available on their own.

Strategy
Your growth goal and what limits it, the customers to focus on first, how you win them, what to build to get there, and the growth moves that fit your margins and capacity, ranked by priority.
Plan
A roadmap of growth objectives and KPI targets, with the channel mix, messaging and budget allocation for each growth move, and a measurement plan.
Execution
The agreed growth moves, such as campaigns, landing pages or lifecycle flows, built and managed end to end. Each one is tested before budget scales, with KPIs reviewed monthly and the budget split every quarter.
Scope
The channels, the pace and the size of the engagement are shaped around your business model, market, growth stage and budget.

Or hire me for one part

Pick one service, or combine them. Each one is scoped to what your business needs.

Audit

Growth audit

Where your growth comes from today, what limits the next stage, and whether your margins support it.

I review every channel, your tracking, unit economics and customer data. You get the gap to your growth goal, the main constraint in your revenue, your break even points, a check on tracking, margins and capacity, and a ranked list of what to fix first.

Project · Fixed scope

Research

Audience research and ICP

Who buys from you, what moves them to switch, and the moments that bring them to market.

I research your market and search data and analyse sales calls or transcripts to define your ideal customer, the moments they start looking, why they switch and the words they use. You also get a map of where to reach them.

Project

Acquisition

Paid media setup and management

Campaigns that reach future buyers and capture the demand that already exists, on tracking you can trust.

I audit and structure your accounts on Google, Meta, LinkedIn and Microsoft Ads, then launch, test and report every result openly. Before budget moves between channels, incrementality tests show the extra revenue your ads bring in and whether it is profitable.

Setup project · Weekly management

Conversion

Hooks, ad copy and landing pages

Messaging that answers what holds your buyers back, so more of your existing traffic converts.

I define your positioning and write copy for each audience, grounded in behavioural psychology. Your landing pages get an audit that names what blocks conversion, and a rebuild on the same research.

Project · Testing retainer available

Retention

Lifecycle and CRM

Flows that move leads to a sale and customers to their next purchase, measured on revenue.

I build nurture, onboarding, adoption and win back flows in HubSpot or your CRM, and measure each one with an incrementality test against a holdout group, so you see what every flow adds.

Build project · Optimisation retainer available

New product growth

Product and feature launches

A new product or feature that reaches the right buyers, gets adopted, and keeps selling after launch day.

I position the launch against what your buyers use today, assess which channels fit your business and market, and start with the ones most likely to work. From launch day, I run multiple test rounds to understand your audiences at a deeper level, then scale what drives sales and adoption.

Project · Launch plan and first 90 days
