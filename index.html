Or hire me for one part

Pick one service, or combine them. Each one is scoped to what your business needs.

Audit

Growth audit

Where your growth comes from today, what limits the next stage, and whether your margins support it.

  • Review of every channel, your tracking and customer data
  • Your unit economics and break even points
  • The main constraint between you and your growth goal
  • A ranked list of what to fix first

Research

Audience research and ICP

Who buys from you, what moves them to switch, and the moments that bring them to market.

  • Market, search data and sales call analysis
  • Your ideal customer profile
  • The moments buyers start looking and why they switch
  • The words they use, and where to reach them

Acquisition

Paid ads setup and management

A clear account structure on each channel, built around how your buyers find and choose you.

  • Setup of new accounts or restructure of running ones on Google, Meta, LinkedIn and Microsoft Ads
  • Search structured by intent, social by audience and behaviour
  • Spend read against your own break-even point
  • Test rounds before budget scales
  • Monthly performance review against your targets

Conversion

Hooks, ad copy and landing pages

Messaging that answers what holds your buyers back, so more of your existing traffic converts.

  • Positioning and copy for each audience
  • Hooks grounded in behavioural psychology
  • Messages tested in rounds, then built into one core message
  • Landing page audit naming what blocks conversion
  • A rebuild based on the same research

Retention

Lifecycle and CRM

Flows that move leads to a sale and customers to their next purchase, measured on revenue.

  • Nurture, onboarding, adoption and win back flows
  • Built in HubSpot or your CRM
  • Lead scoring and routing agreed with your sales team
  • Behavioural triggers across the customer journey

New product growth

Product and feature launches

A new product or feature that reaches the right buyers, gets adopted, and keeps selling after launch day.

  • Competitor analysis against current alternatives
  • Positioning on where your product wins, and for whom
  • The channels that fit your business and market
  • Test rounds from launch day
  • Scaling what drives sales and adoption
