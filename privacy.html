What we collect and why

Website analytics, only with your consent. When you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how you use this site: the pages you view, the links you click (for example a booking link or a link to one of the labs), your device and browser type, and your approximate location at city level. Google Analytics does not store full IP addresses. We use this to understand which parts of the site are useful. If you decline, no analytics cookies are placed. Legal basis: your consent.

Booking a call. When you book an introductory call, you do so through Calendly, which processes the details you enter, such as your name, email address and any notes. We use these to prepare and hold the call and to send a proposal. Legal basis: steps you ask for before entering into an agreement.

Email. When you email us, we use your message and contact details to reply and, where relevant, to work together. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your request, or the agreement we enter into with you.

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data for automated decisions.
